Privacy questions

This Privacy Policy explains how handles information associated with use of this website. The site is an independent digital privacy and online security information aggregator and does not provide direct security services.

Overview

+

This website provides general educational information about digital privacy, online security and related service options.

We aim to collect only the information that is reasonably necessary to operate the website, respond to messages and maintain basic website functionality.

Independent information platform

Information available on this site should not be treated as a guarantee of specific security, privacy or technical outcomes. Third-party services should be evaluated independently before use.

Information we receive

+

The types of information associated with your use of this website may depend on how you interact with it.

  • Information you voluntarily provide through the contact form, such as your name, email address, subject and message.
  • Basic technical information that may be processed automatically by a hosting provider, such as browser type, device type, approximate request time or IP-related network information.
  • Preference information stored locally in your browser, such as your cookie-consent choice.
  • Information generated when you follow links to third-party websites, subject to the privacy practices of those third parties.

This website does not request telephone numbers through its contact forms.

How information is used

+

Information associated with use of the site may be used for purposes including:

  • Responding to messages submitted through the contact form.
  • Operating, maintaining and improving website functionality.
  • Detecting abusive, automated or suspicious requests.
  • Remembering website preferences where local browser storage is used.
  • Complying with applicable legal obligations where required.

Information submitted through the contact form is not intended to be used for unrelated advertising purposes.

Third-party resources

+

The website may provide links or references to independent third-party websites, resources, products or service providers.

Once you leave this website, information may be processed according to the terms and privacy practices of the third party you choose to visit.

Check provider policies

Features, availability, pricing, data practices and security claims should be confirmed directly with the relevant provider before making a decision.

Data security

+

Reasonable administrative and technical measures may be used to reduce the risk of unauthorised access to information associated with this website.

However, no website, network or electronic transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

You should avoid submitting passwords, authentication codes, financial credentials or other highly sensitive information through the contact form.

Information retention

+

Information submitted through the contact form may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to the request, maintain relevant records or satisfy legitimate administrative or legal requirements.

Preference information stored locally in your browser may remain until you clear browser storage or change the relevant preference.

Your choices

+

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights or choices concerning personal information associated with your use of the website.

  • You may choose not to submit the contact form.
  • You may clear locally stored website preferences through your browser.
  • You may request information about a message you previously submitted, where reasonably identifiable.
  • You may request correction or deletion of information where applicable and legally permitted.
