This Cookie Policy explains how Privora uses cookies, local browser storage and similar technologies to support essential website functionality and remember your preferences.

Overview

+

This website is designed to use a limited amount of browser storage.

The site's cookie preference card allows you to accept or decline storage preferences. Your choice is remembered locally in your browser so the same notice does not need to appear every time you visit.

Minimal storage approach

The current website implementation does not require advertising cookies or cross-site behavioural tracking cookies to provide its core informational functionality.

Cookies & browser storage

+

Cookies are small pieces of information that a website can store through a browser. Similar technologies, such as local storage, can also remember information directly within your browser.

These technologies can be used for many different purposes, including remembering preferences, keeping sessions active, measuring website activity or supporting personalised services.

On this website, local browser storage is used primarily to remember the choice you make in the cookie preference notice.

Storage we currently use

+

The following browser storage may be used by the current website implementation.

Name Technology Purpose Duration
privora_cookie_preference_v1 Local Storage Remembers whether the website preference notice was accepted or declined. Until browser site data is cleared or the preference is reset.

Hosting infrastructure may also use technically necessary mechanisms to deliver website files, maintain network security or process web requests.

Your cookie preference

+

When the website does not already have a stored preference, a compact cookie notice appears near the bottom of the page.

You can choose either:

  • Accept — the website records that you accepted the preference notice.
  • Decline — the website records that you declined the preference notice.

In both cases, the choice itself may be saved locally so the notice remains dismissed on future visits from the same browser.

Declining does not block navigation

Declining the preference notice does not prevent you from navigating between the website's informational pages or using its ordinary content.

Essential website storage

+

Some technical processing may be required for a website to function securely and reliably.

This may include infrastructure involved in:

  • Delivering requested website pages and files.
  • Maintaining website and network security.
  • Preventing abusive or automated requests.
  • Remembering a preference that you explicitly selected.

Third-party websites

+

This website may contain links to independent third-party websites or service providers.

If you follow one of those links, the third party may use its own cookies, analytics, browser storage or other technologies.

Those technologies are controlled by the third party and are subject to its own privacy and cookie policies.

External policies apply

Before using a third-party privacy or security provider, review its current cookie and privacy practices directly on the provider's website.

Managing browser storage

+

Most modern browsers allow you to inspect, remove or restrict website data.

  • You can clear stored website data from your browser settings.
  • You can remove local storage associated with this website.
  • You can configure your browser to restrict some cookie technologies.
  • Clearing website storage may cause the cookie preference notice to appear again.

Browser controls vary by browser, operating system and device.

Changes to this policy

+

This Cookie Policy may be updated if website functionality, storage technologies or legal requirements change.

If additional non-essential technologies are introduced in the future, the website's notice and this policy may also be updated to explain those changes.

The revision date shown near the top of this page indicates when this policy was most recently updated.
