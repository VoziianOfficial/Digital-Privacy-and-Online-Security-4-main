Practical focus

Small changes. Better control.

Privacy does not have to begin with complicated software. Several useful improvements come from understanding and adjusting the systems you already use.

Browser privacy

Review cookies, website permissions, tracking controls and stored browsing information.

App permissions

Check access to location, contacts, media, camera and microphone features.

Public profiles

Data choices

Connected layers

Privacy moves between devices.

One account may appear across several devices and applications. The privacy settings around each connection can influence the whole picture.

Privacy routine

Review. Reduce. Protect. Repeat.

Review

Identify what information is currently visible or accessible.

Reduce

Remove permissions or shared information that is no longer necessary.

Protect

Strengthen important access and privacy controls.

Repeat

Revisit settings as devices and online habits change.

Share less by default.

Personal information is easier to protect when less of it is distributed across unnecessary services, applications and public profiles.
